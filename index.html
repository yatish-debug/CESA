About Our Association

Cultivating Technical Acumen & Leadership

The Computer Engineering Student Association (CESA) represents the collective ambition and creativity of computer engineers at Godavari College of Engineering, Jalgaon.

Fostering A Culture Of Innovation

CESA acts as the active bridge between classroom academic curriculum and practical industry standards.

Organized entirely by students under the guidance of our Head of Department and faculty mentors, CESA provides a collaborative platform where every student can cultivate coding competence, event management abilities, public communication skills, and collective teamwork.

From intensive 24-hour hackathons and industrial field tours to our signature cultural gatherings and sports leagues, CESA ensures that every computer engineering undergraduate experiences comprehensive, holistic growth throughout their academic journey.

Our Vision

To nurture technically competent, innovative, and ethically grounded computer engineers capable of solving complex societal challenges and leading modern technological transformations.

🚀

Our Mission

To deliver practical learning experiences through peer mentoring, hands-on workshops, state-level hackathons, industry collaborations, and holistic co-curricular events throughout the academic year.
