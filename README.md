# CESA - Computer Engineering Student Association
### Godavari College of Engineering, Jalgaon (GF's GCOEJ)

Official portal for the **Computer Engineering Student Association (CESA)** at **Godavari Foundation's Godavari College of Engineering, Jalgaon**.

---

## 🌟 Visual Theme & Design Alignment

1. **Colors Aligned With Official Logos**:
   - **Official CESA Navy** (`#0D2E5C`): Deep, prestigious collegiate navy from the CESA text and outer seal.
   - **Cerulean Blue** (`#0077CC` / `#0084D1`): Vibrant orbit blue from CESA and the college mechanical cogwheel.
   - **Godavari Crimson Maroon** (`#851D24`): From the iconic central **"G"** monogram of the Godavari College of Engineering seal.
   - **Plain White & Soft Sand Beige Background** (`#FAF9F6`, `#F4F0E8`, `#FFFFFF`): Clean, calm, human, academic atmosphere without dark AI violets, neon cyberpunk glows, or pitch-black geeky themes.
