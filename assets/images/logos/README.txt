HOW TO ADD YOUR OFFICIAL LOGOS:
=========================================
1. College Logo:
   - Place your Godavari College of Engineering logo here and name it:
     "college-logo.png" (or "college-logo.jpg")

2. CESA Logo:
   - Place your CESA logo here and name it:
     "cesa-logo.png" (or "cesa-logo.jpg")

The website is already coded to automatically detect these files! If present, it will immediately display your official image files. If not yet added, it gracefully renders the built-in high-definition institutional vector SVG seals.
